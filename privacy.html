New Location Unlocked

Privacy policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

The short version: where you go and what you photograph stays on your iPhone. We have no servers that receive it, no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Your location

With your permission the app uses your iPhone's location to reveal the places you visit on your map, including in the background if you allow "Always". Locations are processed and stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Your photos

If you allow photo access, the app reads the date and location saved with each photo or video so it can reveal the places you have already been. It never uploads your photos or their contents. When you open a photo from the map, it is loaded from your own library and shown on your device only.

Services the app talks to

  • Map tiles. The map is drawn from tiles downloaded from OpenFreeMap. Like any online map, the tile server sees which area of the map is being viewed and your IP address. No location history, account or identifier is sent with those requests.
  • Place names. When you open a photo, its coordinates are sent to Apple's geocoding service to show the town or city it was taken in, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • TestFlight. If you test a pre-release version, Apple's TestFlight shares crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's terms.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-up.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data, because we never receive it.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores lives on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your map and all of its data. You can turn off location or photo access at any time in iOS Settings.

Changes

If a future version adds features that share data, such as seeing friends' progress, it will ask first and this policy will be updated before that version is released.
