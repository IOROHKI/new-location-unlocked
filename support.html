Testing the beta

In TestFlight, take a screenshot inside the app and tap "Share Beta Feedback", or open TestFlight, choose New Location Unlocked and tap "Send Beta Feedback". It reaches the developer directly with the details needed to fix things.

Everything else

Open an issue on GitHub.

Common questions

The map isn't growing while the app is closed. Open the stats panel and tap "Keep revealing while closed", then choose "Always" for location. Low Power Mode and turning off Background App Refresh reduce background updates.

My photos didn't reveal anything. The app needs full photo access and only uses photos that have a location saved with them.
